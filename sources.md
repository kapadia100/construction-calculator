## Thumb-rule quantities per sq ft of covered area (grey structure)
- qaaf.com.pk: about 20 bricks, 0.40 bags of cement, 3.3 kg of steel, 1.8 cft of sand and 1.2 cft of crush.
- Team Overc's: about 0.39 bags of cement and 1.8 kg of steel per sq ft, with more steel (about 3 kg) for houses with a basement.
- The app's default item library splits these across footings, columns, beams, slab, masonry and plaster. For a 2-storey, 3,000 sq ft house with defaults it gives about 3.1 kg of steel, 0.45 bags of cement and 20 bricks per sq ft.

### Cross-check
Published finishing totals: Rs 1,200–1,800/sq ft standard (amanahbuilders.com, Apr 2026); Rs 1,600–2,400 (civilconstructionguide.com, Sep 2026); Rs 3,200 (acco.com.pk, Jan 2026). The defaults put the B-category finishing rate at Rs 3,300, near the top of these. The BOQ finishing lines for a 2-storey, 3,000 sq ft house now come to about Rs 2,600/sq ft, so lower the B finishing rate in Settings if you want the plain-standard band.

## Category rate update (13 Oct 2026)
Finishing rate per sq ft lowered so the main estimate lines up with the itemised BOQ: B 3,300 -> **2,550**, scaled the same way (x0.773) for A 4,400 -> **3,400** and C 2,200 -> **1,700**. Complete rates are now about A 7,000 / B 5,750 / C 4,500 per sq ft. The B total sits inside the published Rs 5,500–7,000 turnkey band, and B finishing sits between the Rs 1,600–2,400 (civilconstructionguide.com) and Rs 3,200 (acco.com.pk) published finishing figures. For the sample 2-storey, 3,000 sq ft house, the BOQ is now within about 2% of the rate-based estimate.
